Privacy Policy – ABC Adventure

Last updated: November 7, 2026

The short version

ABC Adventure has no accounts and no tracking of its own. We do not ask for or collect personal information from children, and we do not sell data.

What stays on your device

If a name is typed in, and as your child plays, the game saves the name, points, stickers, daily streak and the words they find tricky. This is stored only in this browser on this device (local storage). It is not sent to us or anyone else. To delete it, clear this site’s data in your browser settings. We suggest using a nickname instead of a real name.

Third-party services

The game loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may receive your IP address and device details when the page loads. The website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and time of visit. Words are read aloud using the speech feature built into your device or browser. The optional “Support us with a tip” link opens PayPal, and PayPal’s own privacy policy applies there. We never see payment details. We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that sets no cookies, to count visits and whether an ad loaded, by country; no personal information is stored. To choose which content to show, the game asks a free country-lookup service (country.is, or ipapi.co as a backup) for your approximate country based on your IP address; the country code is kept on your device for 24 hours and nothing else is sent. In some regions the game may show an ad from an advertising network, which may receive device information such as your IP address, and we link to our own printable products on Payhip, where Payhip’s privacy policy applies.

Children

ABC Adventure is made for children ages 3 to 8. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We recommend that a grown-up handles the tip link and the name field.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
